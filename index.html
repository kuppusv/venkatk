Author bio

Writing across home, distance, and identity.

Venkat was born and raised in India and has loved stories for as long as he can remember. Now based in Singapore, he works as a cybersecurity professional while enjoying family life with his wife and two sons.

Mentryyx Game is his third and latest novel, following Two Parallel Lines and Saviour Club. Writing remains a passion he is grateful to pursue, and becoming a storyteller has been a dream he has carried since childhood.

His fiction is shaped by the experience of growing up in India and building a life abroad, blending emotional realism, cultural nuance, and the tensions of modern life into the worlds he creates.
